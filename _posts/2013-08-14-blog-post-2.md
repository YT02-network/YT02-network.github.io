I finished a lab today using malware detection tools offered by websites. I learned that the rabbit hole regarding cybersecurity is a bottomless well. What I did today was the tip of the iceberg, and what awaits me makes me kinda nervous. I used the services provided by the websites to analyze a hash. The hash was an extremely well-known Trojan called Emotet that was fairly simple when it came out in 2014. Now, however, it became a beast, using invasion techniques to hide itself within the system. I used the tools provided by various websites to learn a bit more about the malware, what it did, and how it did it. This was a very eye-opening experience, and it was really fun!!! Although I got confused about how to navigate the websites and sometimes got overwhelmed by the amount of information these tools provide, it was a really enjoyable and great learning experience.
